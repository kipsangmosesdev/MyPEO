XbomaSwift

A PropTech platform designed to make rental discovery and relocation in Kenya more connected—from finding a home to getting movers.

The problem

Traditional house hunting can make it difficult to find suitable vacant homes, compare options, and plan the move in one place.

My contribution

I designed and developed the platform concept and its full-stack capabilities, combining map-based search, tailored recommendations, landlord tools, and relocation discovery.

Product approach

  • Locate vacant rentals within 5 km and recommend homes by budget, location, amenities, and availability.
  • Use Google Maps for doorstep navigation and nearby mover discovery.
  • Support landlord analytics, ratings and reviews, multi-image listings, and tenant and landlord accounts.
  • Plan monetization through subscriptions and tokens with M-Pesa mobile payments.

Technical direction

Python and Django backend, PostgreSQL data layer, JavaScript interface, and Google Maps integration. The architecture is cloud-ready, with room for future AI expansion.

SakaTalent

A working MVP for grassroots athletics in Kenya that helps athletes build a verified performance record and makes talent easier to discover.

The problem

Emerging athletes need a clearer way to present their performances and be found by people looking for talent across events and counties.

My contribution

I helped develop the platform with colleagues in the ICT Department. It is a team project; the MVP’s current verification workflow includes a team verifier reviewing submitted times before publication.

Discovery and ranking

  • Athlete profiles capture submitted race performances.
  • Visitors can filter athletes by county, gender, and the 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, or 800 m events.
  • Event-specific averages and rankings compare verified results, with each athlete’s best recorded time available.

Technical direction

The MVP uses Python, Django, and SQLite. PostgreSQL is planned. SakaTalent is intended as an additional visibility channel that complements existing athletics structures.
