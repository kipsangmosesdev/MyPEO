XbomaSwift

A PropTech platform for rental discovery and relocation in Kenya. I designed and developed a connected search experience that helps tenants find, evaluate, navigate to, and move into rental homes.

  • Find vacant rentals within 5 km and get AI recommendations based on budget, location, amenities, and availability.
  • Navigate to a property and discover nearby movers through Google Maps.
  • Give landlords listing analytics; support reviews, M-Pesa subscriptions, token payments, and tenant and landlord accounts.
  • Cloud-ready architecture designed for scale and future AI expansion.

ICT DEPARTMENT TEAM PROJECT02

SakaTalent

A web-based talent discovery and performance-tracking platform for grassroots track athletes in Kenya. Developed with colleagues in the ICT Department, its working MVP gives athletes a public profile and a place to build a verified record of their results.

  • Athletes create profiles and submit race times; the current team verifier reviews results before publication.
  • Discover runners by county, gender, and event across the 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, and 800 m.
  • Compare verified event-specific averages, rankings, and each athlete’s best recorded time.
  • Designed as an additional visibility channel that complements existing athletics structures.
